# Emirati Sign Language (ESL) draft pack

DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED. Only signs with a citable published description are animated. They approximate text and must be reviewed by a Deaf Emirati signer against the official Emirati Sign Language Dictionary (Zayed Higher Organization for People of Determination, https://www.za.gov.ae/Sign-Language-Dictionary) before any real use. ESL differs between emirates; the dictionary is the unified reference.

- **Coffee** (قهوة): Draft from a published description: a held imaginary cup is shaken (not lifted to the lips). Reference: https://www.euronews.com/2020/07/03/why-isn-t-there-one-unified-sign-language-in-the-middle-east-region
- **Dubai** (دبي): Draft from a published description: horizontal G hand, palm sideways, nose tip between index finger and thumb. Reference: https://www.handspeak.com/word/7784/
